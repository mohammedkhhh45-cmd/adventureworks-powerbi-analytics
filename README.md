# 📊 AdventureWorks Power BI Financial & Sales Analytics Dashboard


An end-to-end interactive Power BI report analyzing sales performance, revenue trends, regional profitability, customer demographics, and order management for **AdventureWorks**.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

* **Total Revenue:** $24.91M
* **Net Profit:** $10.46M (~42% Profit Margin)
* **Total Orders:** 25K (84K units sold)
* **Active Customer Base:** 17K active users out of 18K registered

---

## 📈 Dashboard Overview & Key Insights

### 1. Sales & Revenue Analysis (`Sales` View)
* **Profitability:** Generated $24.91M in total revenue against $14.46M in costs.
* **Growth Trend:** Strong YoY revenue and profit growth from 2020 to 2021, plateauing through 2022.
* **Return Rate:** Maintained a stable low return rate of **2.15%**.

### 2. Orders & Product Distribution (`Orders` View)
* **Peak Seasonality:** Order volumes consistently peak in mid-year (June) and year-end (December), with a seasonal drop around July.
* **Order Patterns:** **71.86%** of orders occur during workdays vs. **28.14%** on weekends.
* **Category Breakdown:** Accessories lead total order counts, followed by Bikes and Clothing.

### 3. Geographic Performance (`Region` View)
* **Top Market:** The United States generated the highest quantity sold (~30K units), followed by Australia, Canada, UK, Germany, and France.
* **Profit Share by Continent:**
  * **North America:** $4.12M (39.42%)
  * **Europe:** $3.26M (31.16%)
  * **Pacific:** $3.08M (29.42%)

### 4. Customer Demographics (`Customer` View)
* **Target Profile:** Average customer age is **64.42 years**, with the primary segments holding Bachelor's or Partial College degrees.
* **Top Customer:** Jordan Turner leads in revenue contribution ($15.57K).
